I have spent more than twenty years building software, most often data-heavy
applications that I owned from the database to the interface, in scientific,
public health, geospatial and business settings. My roots in AI go back to my
master's with [Yoshua Bengio](https://yoshuabengio.org), where I worked on
neural language models. Today I teach AI and software engineering at
[TÉLUQ](https://www.teluq.ca), and I build tools that connect language models to
real systems. My code is on [GitHub](https://github.com/cjauvin), and my older
writing on my [first blog](https://cjauvin.blogspot.com).
