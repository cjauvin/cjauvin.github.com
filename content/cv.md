I have spent more than twenty years building software, most often data-heavy
applications that I owned from the database to the interface, in scientific,
public health, geospatial and business settings. My roots in AI go back to my
master's with Yoshua Bengio, where I worked on neural language models. Today I
teach AI and software engineering at TÉLUQ, and I build tools that connect
language models to real systems.
