Projects

Small things I built for the pleasure of it, mostly browser toys. They are all open source.

Cabinet of Distilled Pixels

Thirty classic arcade mechanics, from 1962 to today, each boiled down to its rules and running live on a small grid in the same five colours. You can see at a glance how Pong grew into Breakout, or what Pac-Man and Bomberman have in common.

Visit ↗ · Source canvas

The Esterházy Palace, Eisenstadt

A walkable reconstruction of Schloss Esterházy and its square, after an unsigned 19th-century coloured engraving, with the whole scene rendered as an engraving. Follow the harpsichord to the great hall where Haydn’s orchestra played.

Walk in ↗ · Source three.js

Naqsh-e Jahan Square, Isfahan

An explorable model of the royal square of Shah Abbas, with the Shah Mosque, the Sheikh Lotfollah Mosque, Ali Qapu and the bazaar gate. Every tile pattern and every note of the Persian soundscape is generated in the browser, with no asset files.

Walk in ↗ · Source three.js

Solo

A free solo climbing game for the browser. One 62 m route, no rope. Your forearms tire, holds can be loose, and if you let go with both hands, you fall. Desktop browser, mouse and keyboard required.

Play ↗ · Source three.js

Learn Lean

An interactive, puzzle-driven course in Lean 4: programming first, proofs second, with the punchline that they were the same thing all along. 73 exercises across 8 chapters, each with a test that goes red until you solve it, plus three browser applets for the things that are hard to see from inside a text editor.
